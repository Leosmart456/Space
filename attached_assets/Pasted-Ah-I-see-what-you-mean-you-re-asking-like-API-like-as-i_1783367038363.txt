### 📡 Example API Calls for Stock Market News

1. **Alpha Vantage (News Endpoint)**
```http
GET https://www.alphavantage.co/query?function=NEWS_SENTIMENT&tickers=SPACEX&apikey=YOUR_API_KEY
```
- Returns recent news articles and sentiment scores for SpaceX.

2. **Finnhub**
```http
GET https://finnhub.io/api/v1/company-news?symbol=SPACEX&from=2026-06-10&to=2026-07-01&token=YOUR_API_KEY
```
- Pulls company-specific news between given dates.

3. **IEX Cloud**
```http
GET https://cloud.iexapis.com/stable/stock/SPACEX/news?token=YOUR_API_KEY
```
- Provides latest SpaceX-related headlines.

4. **Polygon.io**
```http
GET https://api.polygon.io/v2/reference/news?ticker=SPACEX&apiKey=YOUR_API_KEY
```
- Retrieves financial news tagged to SpaceX.

5. **MarketStack**
```http
GET http://api.marketstack.com/v1/news?access_key=YOUR_API_KEY&symbols=SPACEX
```
- Offers stock market news feeds.

6. **Financial Modeling Prep (FMP)**
```http
GET https://financialmodelingprep.com/api/v4/company-news/SPACEX?apikey=YOUR_API_KEY
```
- Company news tied to SpaceX ticker.

7. **Quandl (Nasdaq Data Link)**
```http
GET https://data.nasdaq.com/api/v3/datasets?query=SpaceX&api_key=YOUR_API_KEY
```
- Pulls datasets and related financial info.

8. **NewsAPI.org**
```http
GET https://newsapi.org/v2/everything?q=SpaceX+IPO&apiKey=YOUR_API_KEY
```
- General news search, filtered for IPO coverage.

9. **Yahoo Finance (Unofficial)**
```http
GET https://query1.finance.yahoo.com/v7/finance/quote?symbols=SPACEX
```
- Provides quote and related news.

10. **Bloomberg (limited feeds)**
```http
GET https://api.bloomberg.com/news?query=SpaceX&apikey=YOUR_API_KEY
```
- Market headlines, though Bloomberg’s open API is more restricted.

---

👉 These are **example endpoints** — you’d need to replace `SPACEX` with the actual ticker symbol once it’s officially listed (likely something like `SPX` or `SPACEX` depending on the exchange).
